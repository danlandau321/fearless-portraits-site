# Fearless Portraits

## What it is

The website for *Fearless Portraits*, my book of hand-drawn ink portraits of 37 women
who defied expectations and changed the world — revolutionaries, scientists, business
leaders, artists, and activists. Each portrait is paired with a short biography.

It's a single-page site at [danlandau.net](https://danlandau.net) with a gallery of
portraits from the book, links to buy it in hardcover and paperback, a bit about me,
and a way to get in touch.

## Why I built it

I draw portraits in ink on paper maps, where the geography adds context and texture to
each subject. I made the book for my daughters, to show them the limitless possibilities
for their futures. The site is how people find the book and see the work.

## What it's built with

Plain HTML, CSS, and jQuery — no build step. The design is based on the
[Big Picture](https://html5up.net/big-picture) template by HTML5 UP (CCA 3.0), with
custom styling on top. Hosted on GitHub Pages with a custom domain.
